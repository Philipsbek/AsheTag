How to Set Up Google Business Profile for Your Malaysian Business

A complete Google Business Profile puts your business on Maps and in local search — free. Here’s how to set it up properly and make it work.

If you do one free thing for your local visibility this month, make it your Google Business Profile. It’s what populates the map and the business panel when someone searches your name or category — and most Malaysian businesses leave it half-finished.

The setup steps

  • Claim & verify your business (usually by postcard, phone or email).
  • Complete every field — category, hours, services, description.
  • Add real photos — storefront, team, products.
  • Link your website — ideally a fast, relevant page.
  • Collect reviews and reply to them.

Keep it alive

A profile isn’t set-and-forget. Post updates, keep hours current (especially around public holidays), and answer questions. Activity signals to Google that you’re a real, active business.

A complete profile can outrank a bigger competitor’s neglected one. Effort beats size here.

Pair it with your site

Your profile and website reinforce each other. Make sure your site loads fast and matches your listing details. Read our local SEO guide for the full picture.
