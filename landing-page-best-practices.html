Landing Page Best Practices for Malaysian Campaigns

Running ads to your homepage wastes clicks. A focused landing page with one goal converts far better. Here’s how to build one that works.

If you’re running Google or Facebook ads in Malaysia and sending clicks to your homepage, you’re leaking budget. A homepage has many jobs; a landing page has exactly one — and that focus is why it converts.

The anatomy of a landing page

  • One goal — book, buy, or enquire. Nothing else.
  • A headline that matches the ad that brought them.
  • Proof — testimonials, results, guarantees.
  • A single, repeated CTA.
  • Minimal navigation — don’t invite them to wander off.

Match the message

If your ad promised “free consultation”, the landing page headline should say exactly that. Mismatch between ad and page is a top reason campaigns underperform.

A landing page that gives visitors five choices gets far fewer conversions than one that gives them a single obvious one.

Speed and mobile still rule

Ad traffic is mostly mobile and impatient. Keep the page fast — check it on PageSpeed Insights — and the form short. We build campaign landing pages as part of our web design services; see transparent pricing.
