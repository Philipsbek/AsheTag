How Much Does a Website Cost in Malaysia? (2026 Price Guide)

Website design pricing in Malaysia ranges from around RM1,200 for a simple site to RM8,000+ for a fully custom build. Here’s exactly what drives the price.

One of the first questions every Malaysian business owner asks is simple: how much does a website cost in Malaysia? The honest answer is that it depends — but the ranges are more predictable than most people expect. In 2026, a professional website in Malaysia typically costs between RM1,200 and RM8,000+, and the final figure comes down to a handful of clear factors.

What actually drives the price

Price scales with scope. A three-page brochure site for a startup is a very different build from a 15-page site with a blog, booking system and custom design. The main cost drivers are:

  • Number of pages — more pages means more design and content work.
  • Design complexity — template-based vs fully custom layouts.
  • Features — contact forms, booking, e-commerce, integrations.
  • Copywriting and images — done for you or supplied by you.
  • SEO and speed work — basic on-page SEO is often included; advanced work costs more.

Typical package ranges

At AsheTag, our transparent pricing follows three tiers that mirror how most Malaysian SMEs buy: a Launch package (RM1,200–RM1,800) for startups and personal brands, a Growth package (RM2,000–RM3,500) for growing businesses, and an Authority package (RM4,000–RM8,000+) for established companies that want a premium, fully custom presence.

Cheap is not the same as affordable. A RM500 site that never brings in an enquiry is expensive; a RM2,000 site that wins customers pays for itself.

What about ongoing costs?

Beyond the build, budget for hosting and a domain (often RM200–RM600/year combined) and optional website maintenance to keep the site secure and updated. Hosting usually isn’t bundled into the design fee, but we’re happy to recommend reliable providers. For the technical SEO foundations that help you rank, Google’s own Google Search Essentials is a good reference.

If you’re comparing quotes, make sure you’re comparing like for like — pages, revisions, support period and whether content is included. Our website design is scoped transparently so there are no surprises.
