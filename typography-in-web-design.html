Typography in Web Design: Readability That Converts

Most of a website is words, so typography matters more than most people realise. The right type choices make your site readable, credible and easy to act on.

Strip away the images and most of your website is text. That makes typography one of the most important — and most overlooked — parts of web design. Good type is invisible; bad type quietly pushes people away.

The rules that matter

  • Readable size — body text around 16–18px on the web.
  • Comfortable line length — not too wide to track.
  • Clear hierarchy — headings that guide the eye.
  • Limited fonts — one or two families, used consistently.

Hierarchy guides attention

Size, weight and spacing tell visitors what’s important and in what order. A clear hierarchy lets scanners (which is everyone) still absorb your message.

If readers have to work to read it, most won’t. Clarity is a conversion feature.

Performance and pairing

Choose web fonts that load fast and pair a characterful heading font with a highly legible body font. It ties into your wider brand identity. See our website design for how we apply it.
